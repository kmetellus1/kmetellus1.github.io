About

I'm Kirk. For more than ten years I worked in physical security across New York as a Security Guard and Peace Officer: monitoring cameras, controlling access, and documenting incidents.

At CSX I monitored digital control and signal systems in a 24/7 operations environment, catching anomalies in real time and escalating incidents. Today I support enterprise users at Google's NYC office through Astreya, and I'm building toward systems and security work through certifications and hands-on labs.
